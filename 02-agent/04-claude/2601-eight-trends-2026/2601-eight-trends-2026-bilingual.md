How engineering teams are shifting from writing code to orchestrating agents. Eight trends, real-world case studies, and predictions for 2026.

工程团队如何从编写代码转向编排智能体（orchestrating agents）。八大趋势、真实案例研究，以及对 2026 年的预测。

How is AI changing the way software gets built—and what should engineering leaders expect in 2026? We analyzed the patterns emerging across the industry.

AI 正在如何改变软件的构建方式--工程负责人在 2026 年又该期待什么？我们分析了整个行业中正在浮现的模式。

Coding agents are now collaborators.

编码智能体（coding agents）如今已成为协作者。

In 2025, engineering teams discovered AI could handle entire implementation workflows: writing tests, debugging failures, navigating complex codebases. In 2026, we predict these capabilities will expand significantly.

2025 年，工程团队发现 AI 可以承接完整的实现工作流：编写测试、调试失败、在复杂代码库中穿行。我们预测，到 2026 年这些能力将显著扩展。

Our new report identifies eight trends we predict will define agentic coding this year, organized into three categories: foundation trends that change how development happens, capability trends expanding what agents accomplish, and impact trends affecting business outcomes.

我们的新报告识别出八大趋势，我们预测它们将定义今年的 agentic coding（智能体化编码），并归纳为三大类：改变开发方式的基础趋势（foundation trends）、拓展智能体能力边界的能力趋势（capability trends），以及影响业务成果的影响趋势（impact trends）。

The organizations pulling ahead aren't removing engineers from the loop, they're making engineer expertise count where it matters most.

领先的组织并没有把工程师移出循环，而是让工程师的专业能力在最关键的地方发挥价值。

## 我们正在看到什么（What we're seeing）

The software development lifecycle is undergoing one of its most significant changes since the graphical user interface. Engineers are shifting from writing code to coordinating agents that write code, focusing their own expertise on architecture, system design, and strategic decisions.

软件开发生命周期正在经历自图形用户界面（GUI）以来最重大的变革之一。工程师正从编写代码转向协调那些编写代码的智能体，把自己的专业能力聚焦于架构、系统设计和战略性决策。

A critical nuance has emerged from studying how developers actually work with AI: this transformation relies on active collaboration.

通过研究开发者实际如何与 AI 协作，一个关键的细微之处浮现出来：这场转型依赖的是主动协作。

Research from our Societal Impacts team reveals that while developers use AI in roughly 60% of their work, they report being able to "fully delegate" only 0-20% of tasks. AI serves as a constant collaborator, but using it effectively requires supervision, validation, and human judgment.

来自我们 Societal Impacts（社会影响）团队的研究显示：开发者大约 60% 的工作中会用到 AI，但他们报告称只有 0-20% 的任务能够"完全委托"（fully delegate）。AI 扮演着持续协作的伙伴，但要有效使用它，离不开监督、验证和人类判断。

## 实践中的样貌（What this looks like in practice）

Organizations across industries are putting these patterns into practice, balancing agent autonomy with human oversight to ship faster without sacrificing quality.

各行业的组织正在把这些模式付诸实践，在智能体自主性与人类监督之间取得平衡，从而在不牺牲质量的前提下更快交付。

Rakuten engineers tested Claude Code on a complex technical task: implementing an activation vector extraction method in vLLM, a 12.5-million-line codebase. Claude Code finished the job in seven hours of autonomous work, achieving 99.9% numerical accuracy.

Rakuten 的工程师在一个复杂的技术任务上测试了 Claude Code：在 vLLM 这个 1250 万行代码的代码库中实现一种激活向量提取（activation vector extraction）方法。Claude Code 经过七个小时的自主工作完成了任务，数值准确率达到 99.9%。

TELUS teams created over 13,000 custom AI solutions while shipping engineering code 30% faster, saving over 500,000 hours total.

TELUS 的团队创建了超过 13,000 个定制 AI 解决方案，同时工程代码交付速度提升 30%，累计节省超过 50 万小时。

Zapier achieved 89% AI adoption across their entire organization with 800+ agents deployed internally.

Zapier 在整个组织内实现了 89% 的 AI 采用率，内部部署了 800 多个智能体。

## 前进之路（The path forward）

For organizations planning their 2026 priorities, four areas demand immediate attention: mastering multi-agent coordination, scaling human-agent oversight through AI-automated review, extending agentic coding beyond engineering teams, and embedding security architecture from the earliest stages.Organizations that treat agentic coding as a strategic priority will define what becomes possible.

对于正在规划 2026 年优先事项的组织，有四个领域需要立即关注：掌握多智能体协调（multi-agent coordination）、通过 AI 自动化审查扩展人机监督规模、把 agentic coding 推广到工程团队之外，以及从最早期就嵌入安全架构。那些把 agentic coding 视为战略优先级的组织，将定义未来可能的边界。
